1 Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Dragoster Studios ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the Ancient Ludo: Snake & Ladder mobile game application ("the App") available on Google Play.

The App itself does not ask for an account, a name, an email address, or any personal details — you can pick up and play immediately. The App does show advertising through Google AdMob to keep the game free, and Google AdMob collects your device's advertising identifier to do that. That is the only data connected to this App.

🛡️ Short version: No accounts, no personal info, no location, no analytics. The App shows ads via Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID to serve and measure those ads. Your game progress and settings stay on your device only.

2 Information We Collect

The only information associated with the App is collected on our behalf by our advertising partner, Google AdMob, in order to display ads:

✓ Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / AAID)

We never see or store this identifier ourselves — it is read from your device and sent directly to Google as part of each ad request. We do not operate any servers of our own, and nothing about your gameplay is sent to us.

3 Information We Do NOT Collect

Beyond the advertising identifier described above, the App does not collect any of the following:

✕ Personal information ✕ Email addresses ✕ Names or usernames ✕ Precise location ✕ Usage analytics ✕ Crash reports ✕ Purchase history ✕ Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone

The App has no login system, no in-app purchases, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of any kind.

4 Advertising

The App displays advertising supplied by Google AdMob (part of Google's Mobile Ads platform) to support free play. Two ad formats are used:

✓ Banner ads ✓ Interstitial (full-screen) ads shown between matches

To serve and measure these ads, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier and other technical signals (such as general device and network information) as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We do not control how Google's ad systems use this data beyond serving ads inside this App, and we do not receive or store it ourselves.

5 Your Ad Choices

You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising identifier at any time, independently of this App, through your device settings:

Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete/reset advertising ID Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

These settings are controlled by Android and Google Play Services, not by us — turning them on will still allow ads to appear, but limits how they may be personalized.

6 Internet & Permissions

The App uses your internet connection solely to request and display ads. Core gameplay — rolling dice, moving pieces, playing against the built-in computer opponent — works the same with or without a connection; only ads require one to load.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or stored files. The only permissions used are the standard ones Android and Google's Mobile Ads SDK require to show ads and keep the app running (such as internet access and the advertising ID permission).

7 Third-Party Services

The only third-party service integrated into the App is:

✓ Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)

We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK, Crashlytics, or any other analytics, tracking, or mediation network.

8 Children's Privacy

Ancient Ludo: Snake & Ladder is a general-audience board game and is not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age — the only data involved is the advertising identifier described above, handled by Google AdMob for ad delivery.

If you believe a child has used this App and you have concerns about advertising shown to them, please contact us using the details below and we will assist however we can.

9 Data Security

Your game progress and preferences (such as background music volume) are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted anywhere. The only data leaving your device is the advertising identifier sent to Google AdMob as described in Section 4, which is handled under Google's own security and privacy practices.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App changes — for example, if we add new features or ad formats. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
