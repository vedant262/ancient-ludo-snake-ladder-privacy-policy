Privacy Policy

Ancient Ludo: Snake & Ladder  ·  Developer: Vedant  ·  Last updated: May 28, 2026

1. Overview

Ancient Ludo: Snake & Ladder is a completely offline, single-device board game. This Privacy Policy explains clearly what data we collect — and the short answer is: none at all. Your privacy is fully protected because we have nothing to protect it from.

2. No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, age, gender, email, phone number)
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, Advertising ID)
  • Location data (GPS or network-based)
  • Camera, microphone, or contacts access
  • Usage statistics, analytics, or crash reports
  • Social media profiles or linked accounts
  • Payment or financial information

3. No Internet Access

This app does not require an internet connection to function and does not communicate with any remote server, cloud service, or external database at any time. All game logic runs entirely on your device.

4. No Ads or Third-Party SDKs

This app contains no advertisements of any kind — no banner ads, no video ads, no rewarded ads. We have not integrated any third-party advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Firebase, Facebook SDK, etc.).

5. Local Storage

The app may save game settings and preferences locally on your device using standard Android storage. This data exists only on your device, is never uploaded anywhere, and can be fully removed by uninstalling the app.

6. Children's Privacy

This game is suitable for all ages including children. Because we collect no data whatsoever, there is no risk to children's privacy. We fully comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and Google Play Families Policy.

7. Permissions

This app requests no special Android permissions. It does not ask for access to:

  • Camera or microphone
  • Contacts or call logs
  • Location services
  • Storage beyond its own local app folder
  • Internet or network state

8. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be published at this same URL with an updated date. Since we collect no data, any changes would only reflect changes in the app's features.
